## Stack

## Purpose

greghermo.com is Greg Hermo's personal home base: one place that says who he is, what he builds, and what he believes, with his writing alongside it.

## Users and jobs

- **Primary:** people who already know of Greg, or just met him (peers, friends, church and business contacts, people in tech), who look him up to understand who he is. Success means they come away knowing his story, his work and his values, and read his posts.
- **Secondary:** recruiters, collaborators or possible clients who arrive the same way. They can reach him through email, LinkedIn or GitHub, but the site isn't built to convert them.
