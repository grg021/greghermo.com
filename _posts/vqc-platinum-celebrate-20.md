December 7, 2015 Victory QC celebrated 20 years of God’s faithfulness. From a small group of faithful and hard-working people and now to thousands, gathering in GT Toyota and Regis Center, Katipunan, bound by the Great Commission, to Honor and God and make disciples.

We enjoyed a world-class show at Every Nation, Fort Bonifacio to honor the leaders and volunteers of the church and celebrate our 20th year anniversary.
