You’re my oasis in the scorching sun\
My second wind in an endless run\
My better half, with you I am complete\
My one and only, no one can compete

Your kind soul, your honest heart\
To you I don’t ever want to part\
I love your smile, I love your diction\
I love your face and all its expression

Your thoughts, your care\
My baby, you are rare\
Your witty remarks, your tactless laugh\
Please continue, I just can’t get enough

In this short life, in this temporary home\
Your love give me strength, it gives me shalom\
Being with you I am content\
Your love alone is my only intent

Everyday of my life, to God I am grateful\
Though it is the days with you, I am most thankful\
For you are my sanctuary, my life reserve\
You are the gift, I can never deserve

To pursue you is my passion\
To love you is my reaction\
I am for you, my life is set\
From the very start, even before we met

I love you, I love you dearly\
you mean the world to me, clearly\
I only hope and I only pray\
That you are also blessed by my in any way

Soar the skies and wander about\
I will go wherever, whenever no doubt\
Travel overseas and coast to coast\
To be in the place I love the most

## The Story Behind

January 16, 2012, we had dinner at Mom and Tina’s, Regis Center, Katipunan. We had a great time and the food was really good. I gave Darla the photo below with the poem I wrote for her printed on the picture. She cried while reading it.

![Dec. 3, 2011 — Darla ❤ Greg @ Taal Volcano PH](/assets/blog/the-place-i-love-the-most/01-taal-volcano.png)

_Dec. 3, 2011 — Darla ❤ Greg @ Taal Volcano PH_

January 15, 2012, we weren't together. I forgot our monthsary! :|

**From Poem to Song** — My friend, Ms. Nevs shared the poem to one of her good friend in Cambodia. After a month, the poem came back to me as a song. Here’s the music video I gave Darla on Feb. 15, 2012.

“The place I love the most” is an original composition. It’s about a love that brings out the best in you.
