The team has been working hard to refine existing features and add ones that make the experience better and more efficient. Here are some key changes that you might like to check-out.

### Store Benchmark

We’ve updated the store benchmark feature to accommodate more metrics — footfall, passer-by, store-front and sales conversion. The date-picker has been updated as well for easier navigation.

![](/assets/blog/november-wrap-up-product-update/01-1-lD2prK8h4BNdXXmWDQ2Z_Q.png)

### Updated Navigation

Inspired by Google Analytics, you can now easily navigate between stores and its corresponding analytics. Some menu items we’re renamed and sorted to ensure a good sense of flow.

![](/assets/blog/november-wrap-up-product-update/02-1-IwhqTRo-38z8HQE9VOvruQ.png)

![](/assets/blog/november-wrap-up-product-update/03-1-ypJzhxs2JX2UbAAL6c184Q.png)

### Visits

The visualization for visits has been updated. We stuck with the more defined and readable bar chart vs the previous line-chart. Check it out.

![](/assets/blog/november-wrap-up-product-update/04-1-ebFvXEptTyeoDsQCgAD8eQ.png)

![](/assets/blog/november-wrap-up-product-update/05-1-kToTpL_YE-pzMiDCGztung.png)

### Change Password

Better late than never, we’ve now added change password and forgot password. Be more secure, change your password now!

![](/assets/blog/november-wrap-up-product-update/06-1-NP38N4tXk5ku02JOQ6ryJw.png)

![](/assets/blog/november-wrap-up-product-update/07-1-f4VQiu74uJ3TnFU7TBijLw.png)

### November Wrap-up

November has been a month of both big and small optimizations and fixes.
